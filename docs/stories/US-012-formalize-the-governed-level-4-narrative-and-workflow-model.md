# US-012 - Formalize the governed level-4 narrative and workflow model

## Story

As an architect, I want the level-4 story framed around governance, human control, and validation flow, so that later slide implementation teaches the right reasons to move beyond level 3.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the PRD calls for BMAD and LangGraph content, when the architecture framing is written, then it explains the limits of level 3, the role of BMAD stages, the role of LangGraph gates, and the human-in-the-loop principle.
2. Given the talk is deck-based and time-boxed, when the workflow model is finalized, then the concepts are simplified into slide-ready sequences and diagrams instead of process-heavy prose.
3. Given the deck must remain deck-first, when the narrative is reviewed, then orchestration is positioned as a governed next step rather than a product in itself.

## Notes

- This story is the architecture bridge between the maturity section and the BMAD/LangGraph slides.
