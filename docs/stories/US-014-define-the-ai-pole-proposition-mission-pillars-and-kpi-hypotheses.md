# US-014 - Define the AI pole proposition, mission, pillars, and KPI hypotheses

## Story

As a product strategist, I want the AI pole proposal articulated as a concrete operating model, so that the closing business case is anchored in mission, roadmap, and measurable outcomes.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the PRD requires a credible AI pole proposal, when the proposition is defined, then it includes urgency, mission, four pillars, phased roadmap assumptions, KPI hypotheses, and contribution pathways.
2. Given the audience includes sponsors and engineers, when the business framing is reviewed, then it balances ambition, governance, and pragmatic starting conditions.
3. Given the deck must remain defensible, when the proposal is finalized, then it avoids promising organizational maturity or business value that the current evidence cannot support.
